# Privacy Policy for Picture Puzzle

**Last updated:** August 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how **Picture Puzzle** ("we", "our", or "the App") handles information when you use our mobile application on Android devices.

By downloading and using Picture Puzzle, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect and How It Is Used

### A. Personal Information
Picture Puzzle does **not** require you to create an account, log in, or provide any personal identifying information (such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address).

### B. Custom Photos and Device Media
The App offers a feature that allows you to create custom puzzles using photos from your device's photo gallery:
- **Local Processing Only:** Selected photos are processed, cropped, and stored **exclusively on your local device**.
- **No Cloud Upload:** Your personal photos and images are **never** uploaded to our servers, shared with third parties, or transmitted across the internet.

### C. Game Progress and Settings
Your game preferences (such as selected difficulty, active theme, sound settings, language preference, and saved puzzle state) are stored locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences.

---

## 2. Third-Party Services and Analytics

To help us maintain, improve, and support the App, we use trusted third-party services provided by Google LLC:

### A. Google AdMob
- We use Google AdMob to display banner advertisements within the App.
- AdMob may collect and process information such as your device's Advertising ID (GAID), IP address, and interaction data to serve non-personalized or personalized advertisements in accordance with Google's policies.
- Learn more about AdMob's privacy practices: [Google AdMob Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

### B. Google Firebase Analytics
- We use Firebase Analytics to understand how players interact with the App (e.g., game starts, completed puzzles, screen views).
- This data is aggregated, anonymized, and used solely to optimize gameplay, performance, and user experience.

### C. Google Firebase Crashlytics
- We use Firebase Crashlytics to monitor application stability and automatically report crashes.
- Crash reports contain diagnostic technical information, such as device model, OS version, stack traces, and crash timestamps. No personal data is included in crash logs.

For more details on Google's privacy practices across Firebase services, please visit:
[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

---

## 3. Data Retention and Deletion

- All local game data and custom puzzles remain on your device until you choose to delete them within the App or uninstall the application.
- Analytics and crash data collected by Google are retained according to Google's standard data retention policies.

---

## 4. Children's Privacy

Picture Puzzle is designed for a general audience of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions). If you believe a child has provided personal information to third-party services via the App, please contact us so we can take necessary actions.

---

## 5. Security

We value your trust in using Picture Puzzle. The security of your data is important to us, and we rely on industry-standard Android security frameworks and trusted Google APIs to safeguard all interactions.
